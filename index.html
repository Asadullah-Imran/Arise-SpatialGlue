💡 Key Experimental Takeaways & Observations

  • Silhouette vs ARI Decoupling: While DEC models dramatically increase the internal cluster silhouette score (from ~0.30 up to 0.528 in E15_S1), biological ARI peaks earlier during pre-training/early Potts epochs (epochs 116-250), demonstrating that unsupervised cluster compaction does not always linearly correlate with manual anatomical annotations.
  • Lymph Node Sub-clustering: In both Human Lymph Node samples (A1 & D1), Plus V2 + DEC / Motif DEC achieved substantial improvements in ARI (+22% to +35% relative gain over Baseline 4Enc-1L), resolving fine follicular structures.
  • Motif DEC Efficiency: Adding spatial motif graph priors reduced runtime by ~13% compared to Plus V2 while preserving state-of-the-art peak ARI (0.6122 peak in E15_S1) and stabilizing seed variability.
